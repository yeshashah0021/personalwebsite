Background

I'm Yesha Shah — a Computer Science student with a Finance minor at Temple University with a love for building things that matter. Born and raised in New Jersey, I grew up one hour from two major cities, which gave me an appreciation for fast-moving environments and diverse perspectives.

My work lives at the intersection of technology, data, and human impact. Whether I'm testing language model architectures in a research lab, building dashboards for multimillion-dollar energy systems, or organizing hackathons with sponsors like AWS and NVIDIA, I bring the same standard of rigor and creativity.

Outside of tech, I'm a trained Kathak dancer with a bachelor's degree from Pracheen Kala Kendra — a discipline that has shaped how I think about precision, rhythm, and storytelling. I'm also an avid foodie (Taco Bell and noodles, always), and I find joy in cooking, yoga, and discovering new cities.
